Obsidian Self-Hosted LiveSync

Seamless, real-time, end-to-end encrypted note synchronization for Obsidian vaults across your laptops, workstations, and mobile devices using Apache CouchDB on pineapple-station.

1 Setup in Obsidian (Step-by-Step)

Connect your Obsidian vault to this server in under two minutes:

1
Install "Self-hosted LiveSync" Plugin:

In Obsidian, navigate to Settings ➔ Community Plugins ➔ Browse ➔ Search for Self-hosted LiveSync (by vorot93) ➔ Click Install, then Enable.

2
Run the Setup Wizard:

Open the plugin settings ➔ Select the Setup Wizard and choose Use CouchDB (or Beginner mode).

3
Copy & Paste Your CouchDB Sync URI:
https://pineapple-station.taildb42a2.ts.net/couchdb/

Enter your CouchDB username, password, and vault database name (e.g. obsidian_vault).

4
Set End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) Passphrase:

Choose a strong passphrase. This encrypts your markdown notes before they ever leave your device. Only you can decrypt them—even the server cannot read your notes in plaintext.

5
Test Connection & Enable LiveSync:

Click Test Connection. Once verified with green checkmarks, enable LiveSync mode so notes sync continuously while you write.

2 Syncing to Mobile (iOS & Android)

You can replicate your entire vault config to your phone or tablet in seconds without typing passwords:

📱 10-Second QR Setup:
1. On your configured desktop Obsidian: Settings ➔ Self-hosted LiveSync ➔ Copy Setup URI (or show QR Code).
2. On your phone: Make sure Tailscale is connected ➔ Install the plugin in mobile Obsidian ➔ Paste the Setup URI or scan the QR code.
3. Enter your E2EE passphrase to unlock and synchronize!
